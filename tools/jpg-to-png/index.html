Why Convert JPG to PNG?

#01

Zero Generational Loss

PNG uses lossless DEFLATE compression, preventing further compression artifacts.

#02

Crisp Text & Illustrations

Ideal for diagrams, logos, and screenshots that degrade under repeated JPEG saving.

#03

Instant Processing

Runs locally in browser RAM without server upload queues or bandwidth throttling.

#04

Free & Unlimited

Convert as many photos as you need without watermarks or account signups.

When to Use PNG

While converting JPG to PNG won't magically recreate transparency, it freezes the image quality so future edits in graphics editors won't cause additional JPEG compression noise.
